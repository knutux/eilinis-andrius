This post will tell the story of Futbolinis.lt — an ambitious project I worked on for more than ten years. Inspired by the spirit of Wikipedia, I hoped to create the most comprehensive, interlinked archive of Lithuanian football history: players, clubs, league seasons, match protocols, even data extracted from digitized newspapers.

Despite support from the Lithuanian Football Federation and a few data-sharing collaborations, the project remained mostly a one-person effort. In the end, it faded not because it failed — but because it was too much for one person, and too quiet to be noticed.

This post will reflect on what I built, why I started, and why I stopped — and how it feels to let go of a project that few people knew existed, but that meant so much to me for a long time.
