In this post, I’ll look back on my early years with Lithuanian Wikipedia — from discovering the project to becoming one of its first major contributors and its very first administrator.

It wasn’t about status. It was about curiosity, freedom, and a sense that something lasting could be built by ordinary people, quietly, one page at a time.

I’ll reflect on the excitement, the loneliness of being early, and the cultural impact Wikipedia had on language, knowledge, and digital identity in Lithuania. Maybe even share a few stories of forgotten edits and moments where it felt like _we were building something no one knew they needed yet_.

This post will be both nostalgic and thoughtful — a tribute to a digital era that now feels oddly distant, but still meaningful.
